From Puck Prep to People Power

Today’s dispatch highlights the relentless science of extraction, the rise of capable travel espresso, and why the future of the industry belongs to its communities.

By Dr. Wallace Lynch Editor in Chief

September 23, 2026

Specialty coffee has always been a beautiful tug-of-war between precise science and profound human connection. Today’s news perfectly captures that duality, proving that our industry is rapidly evolving on both fronts.

On the human side, the major headline comes from Cafe Imports, which has officially transitioned to a 100% employee-owned model via an ESOP. It’s a massive step forward for equitable business structures in the green coffee sector. Meanwhile, Not a Barista brings us the inspiring story of Bryant Toh, a social worker in Singapore brilliantly utilizing coffee as a bridge to build trust and support youth mental health. It’s a powerful reminder that cafes—like the stunning new Bellwood Coffee build-out in Atlanta recently spotlighted by Sprudge—are more than just retail spaces; they are vital community hubs. The Not a Barista team is also currently on the move, reporting from Dehua in the mountainous Fujian province, tracing the global threads that tie this community together at origin.

Back at the bar, the pursuit of extraction perfection continues unabated. Barista Hustle is digging deep into the physics of puck prep this week. They’re exploring how densification creates more spherical coffee particles for exceptionally even water flow, while also breaking down the ongoing mechanics of the RDT (Ross Droplet Technique) versus the AutoComb. It seems academic science is finally catching up to what home baristas have intuitively known about static reduction for two decades.

For those who refuse to leave this precision at home, The Coffee Chronicler takes a look at the booming travel espresso market. With thorough reviews of the flagship Mhw-3bomber Xpress-58 and the surprisingly capable MiiCoffee Eon (boasting a genuine 51mm basket), battery-operated espresso is proving it’s no longer a gimmick. Whether you're pulling shots in a high-end cafe, a youth center, or out of a backpack, the fourth wave continues to push boundaries.
