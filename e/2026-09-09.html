The theatre still has a counter

Stumptown in Lincoln Heights. Cupping tables went back to origin.

By Dr. Wallace Lynch Editor in Chief

September 9, 2026

Stumptown Coffee Roasters is going back to Los Angeles proper, not a suburb of it. The flagship is the historic Lincoln Heights Theatre. Retail and training share a restored venue. Sprudge called it a pioneering roaster returning to the city. The house is old. The work is cups, in the same room as the people who will teach them.

On Wednesday, September 9, 2026, we hold that theatre to one pressure: whether a flagship still sells coffee, or only the restoration around it.

Rocket Espresso presented Rocket X Audi. A cooperation, they said, for exceptional coffee enjoyment. The machine brand sits on a car. In East Java, GCAG Indonesia ran three days of coffee education with young farmers at origin. Charity cupping. Local coffee people. Coffees from around the world. Fourteen countries. Twenty one cities. Months of planning, fundraising, packing coffee. Shipping paused from August 18 through September 5 so the work could happen on the ground. The dispatch is titled From Cupping Tables Around the World, Back to Origin. From seeds to cups. From cups back to origin. The Kingrinder P2 is a manual mill at forty-five dollars. Three hundred thirty grams. Plastic body. Stainless burrs. Natia Simmons scored it 3.8. CoffeeGeek says pour-over clarity that punches above the price. Static clings unless you wet the beans. About twenty-five grams in the hopper. It does not fit inside an AeroPress. Espresso is not the point. You turn the handle.

A restored theatre. A badge on a hood. A table that went home. The fourth wave is not a marquee. It is whether Lincoln Heights and East Java are still in the same sentence: the room is for the cup.
